Estimation Methodology
Building footprint measured from high-res satellite imagery (Maxar/Esri). Power draw estimated via footprint × server density assumptions × PUE. Water usage estimated via cooling tower count × standard evaporation rates × load factor. Thermal signatures cross-referenced from Landsat-9 TIRS (Band 10, 100m resolution).

Estimates carry ±25–35% uncertainty. Companies do not publicly disclose site-level consumption; satellite inference is currently the primary independent verification method.
